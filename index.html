Circle Games – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Circle Games (the “Game”), published by Southeastern Emergency Care, PLLC (“we”, “us”), handles information. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (levels cleared, coins, items owned, best scores) and settings (sound, music, vibration) in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. Deleting the Game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. To deliver, limit, measure and (if you allow it) personalize ads, AdMob may collect information such as:

  • device identifiers, such as Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) or Android’s Advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device type, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data.

This information is collected and processed by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the Game asks for permission before allowing tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalized using your IDFA. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device’s Google settings.
  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: the Game asks for your consent through Google’s consent tool before ads are personalized. You can review your choice from the Game’s settings screen.
  • Remove Ads: buying “Remove Ads” stops full-screen ads. Optional reward ads are only shown if you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, so the Game can unlock it. We do not receive your name, email, or payment information.

Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising and purchase services described above, we do not share information with anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are best handled by Google (for ad data) or by resetting your device’s advertising identifier. You can always contact us and we will help.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
